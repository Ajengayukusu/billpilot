BillPilot — bill autopay that runs itself

Register your fixed payees once. Every deposit into your vault is paid out automatically — by the chain, not by a bot.

connecting to Base Sepolia…

Set it and forget it. Salary lands in the vault → a reactive contract on Reactive Lasna sees the deposit → a callback on Base Sepolia pays every payee their fixed amount. Payees the balance can't cover are skipped and recorded, never blocking the rest. Leftover funds stay in the vault and can be withdrawn any time.

STEP 1 · BASE SEPOLIA

Deposit

You call deposit() on BillVault. It credits your balance and emits DepositReceived.

STEP 2 · REACTIVE LASNA

React

BillPilotReactive is subscribed to that event. It fires instantly and requests a callback via Omni requestCallbackV_1_0.

STEP 3 · BASE SEPOLIA

Get paid out

The callback proxy delivers to BillExecutor, which runs payAll() on the vault. History is written on-chain.
