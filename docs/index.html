Set It and Forget It · Reactive Omni Buildathon

Fixed bills, paid the moment money lands, by the chain itself.

Register your payees once. Every deposit into your vault pays each payee their fixed amount automatically, no bots, no keepers, no manual transfers. Payees the balance can't cover are skipped and recorded on-chain; leftovers stay in the vault, withdrawable any time.

STEP 1 · BASE SEPOLIA

Deposit

deposit() on BillVault credits your balance and emits DepositReceived.

→
STEP 2 · REACTIVE LASNA

React

BillPilotReactive fires instantly and requests the callback via requestCallbackV_1_0.

→
STEP 3 · BASE SEPOLIA

Payout

BillExecutor runs payAll() on the vault. History is written on-chain.
